Cats Are Gods

My adorable kitties

Miisu

At 16 years old, Miisu has mastered the art of finding the coziest spot in the house. Curled up in a soft bed, Miisu looks like a fluffy ball of warmth and comfort. Age has brought wisdom, but none of the charm that makes Miisu such a beloved companion has faded.

Emi

At 14 years old, Emi still turns heads with her striking green eyes and elegant black-and-white coat. Nestled comfortably in her favorite bed, she watches the world with quiet curiosity and timeless grace. Fourteen wonderful years have made Emi a truly treasured member of the family.

Noppa

At just 3 years old, Noppa brings youthful energy and curiosity to every corner of the home. With his bright eyes and snowy white coat, he has already mastered the balance between playtime and naptime. His playful personality makes him a constant source of joy and entertainment.

Täplä

At 3 years old, Täplä brings curiosity, and a touch of mischief wherever she goes. As Noppa's sister, she shares the same youthful spirit, but with a personality all her own. Whether relaxing or exploring every corner of the house, Täplä fills each day with energy and affection.
